2026

  1. Preprint
    Global Evaluation of Congenital Heart Disease-Associated Non-Coding Variants
    Edwin G Peña-Martı́nez, Shreya Sharma, Joshua G Medina-Feliciano, and 24 more authors
    2026
    Preprint
    Abs DOI

    We developed SNP Bind-n-Seq to evaluate >3,000 congenital heart disease-risk variants for allelic binding for the cardiac TFs NKX2-5, GATA4, and TBX5, coupled with a massively parallel reporter assay. We identified 170 variants that exhibit allelic TF binding and 187 that modulate gene expression.
