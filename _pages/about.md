The **Rodríguez-Martínez Laboratory** studies the molecular mechanisms by which transcription factors regulate gene expression in the context of human disease. We are particularly interested in how non-coding genomic regions — enhancers, silencers, and other *cis*-regulatory elements — are decoded by transcription factors to orchestrate cell-type-specific gene programs.

A major focus of the lab is **cardiovascular disease**, where we investigate how transcription factor networks go awry in conditions such as atherosclerosis and heart failure. We combine computational genomics, biochemistry, and cell biology to understand gene regulation at multiple levels: from individual transcription factor–DNA interactions to genome-wide regulatory networks.

Our work aims to uncover fundamental principles of transcription factor biology and to identify new therapeutic targets in cardiovascular and other complex diseases.

**We are recruiting** motivated undergraduate and graduate students. Please email Dr. Rodríguez-Martínez with your CV and a brief statement of your research interests.
