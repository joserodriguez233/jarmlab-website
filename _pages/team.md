## Join the Lab

We are always looking for motivated researchers at all levels. If you are interested in transcription factor biology, non-coding genomics, or computational approaches to understanding cardiovascular disease, please reach out.

- **Prospective graduate students:** Apply through the [UPR Río Piedras Biology graduate program](https://biology.uprrp.edu) and mention JARMlab in your application.
- **Prospective postdocs:** Send a CV, brief description of your research interests, and contact information for three references to [jose.rodriguez233@upr.edu](mailto:jose.rodriguez233@upr.edu).
- **UPR undergraduates:** Email directly to discuss research opportunities in the lab.
